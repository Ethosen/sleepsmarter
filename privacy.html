Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026.

Who uses the app

The Smarter Tonight publishing app is used only by the channel's own team, on the channel's own TikTok account. No member of the public signs in to it and no visitor data is collected on this website: no cookies, no analytics, no forms.

What the app accesses on TikTok

With the account owner's authorisation (TikTok Login Kit), the app receives an access token for that account and the scopes user.info.basic (the account's display name and avatar, to confirm which account is connected) and video.publish (to upload the channel's videos to that account through the Content Posting API).

What is stored, and where

The access and refresh tokens are stored on the channel's own computer, in a file readable only by the team. They are never shared, sold or sent to any third party. No TikTok user data other than the channel's own account information is accessed or stored.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when the account owner revokes the app's access in TikTok's settings (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions) or deletes the token file. Revoking access immediately stops the app.

Contact

Through the TikTok or YouTube account of the channel (SleepSmarter).
